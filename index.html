The Drive

Route

  • From: 321 E 45th St, Indianapolis, IN 46205
  • To: Ohio State Lacrosse Stadium — 630 Irving Schottenstein Dr, Columbus, OH 43210
  • ~178 miles, almost all I-70 East
  • ~2 hr 45 min without a stop · ~3 hr 15 min with one
📍 Open route in Maps

Charging — Model X Long Range

  • 178 miles is well inside a full charge — charge to 90–100% at home Friday night and you can skip the stop entirely
  • You'd arrive with roughly 35–40% left, enough to get to a Columbus charger anytime

If you want to stop anyway

  • Best stop: Buc-ee's Huber Heights, OH — Supercharger on site, ~110 miles in, right off I-70 (exit 38)
  • Backup: Meijer Supercharger — 2507 Chester Blvd, Richmond, IN, ~70 miles in
  • Near the stadium: Superchargers at the Greater Columbus Convention Center, ~3 miles away
